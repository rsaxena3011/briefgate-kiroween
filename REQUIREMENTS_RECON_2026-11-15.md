## Identity and deadline
- Platform: Devpost. Registered 2026-10-26 (boss completed the form himself;
  signed-in page shows "You're registered for this hackathon").
- Submission period: Oct 30 to Dec 4 2026. Deadline Fri Dec 4 2026 2:00 PM PT =
  Dec 5 2026 03:30 IST. Target submission day: ~Nov 24 IST (per the Oct 31
  category decision).
- Prize: $100,000+ in prizes. Overall: 1st $30,000 / 2nd $20,000 / 3rd $10,000.
  Category: Best Resurrection $5,000 / Best Frankenstein $5,000 / Best Skeleton Crew
  $5,000 / Best Costume Contest $5,000. Best Startup Project $10,000. Best Ghost of
  Kiroween Past $2,000. Best Kiro Crew App $1,000. Best Student Project $1,500 x4
  (students only, not us). Best Demo $1,000. Bonus Blog Prize: 2,000 Kiro credits
  to the 100 highest-scoring submissions with a public blog post. Social Blitz Prize:
  3-month Kiro Pro+ to 5 winners (post with #KiroweenHackathon26).
- An eligible project can win one Overall Prize or one Category Prize, not both,
  plus any number of Bonus Prizes.
- Only NEW projects are eligible: built during Oct 30-Dec 4, 2026. Kiroween 2025
  projects and anything started before Oct 30 are not eligible.
- Teams up to 3 members; solo is fine. Kiro credits available to registered
  participants on request (Resources tab).

## Categories (pick one)
- Resurrection: bring dead technology back to life.
- Frankenstein: stitch together a chimera of technologies into one app.
- Skeleton Crew: a lean skeleton code template, proven with two distinct
  applications from the one foundation.
- Costume Contest: any app with a haunting, polished, unforgettable UI.

## What to build
- A working software application that uses Kiro (IDE, CLI, Web, and/or Kiro Crew).
- Category locked 2026-10-31 (boss-delegated pick): FRANKENSTEIN — a brief-vs-final
  video QC gate (see CATEGORY_DECISION_2026-10-31.md and BUILD_SPEC_v1.md).

## What to submit (every field, from the live page)
- Select the category + any Bonus Prizes to be considered for.
- URL to the public GitHub repo (NOT GitLab/BitBucket) for judging and testing:
  must contain all source, assets, and instructions to run; must be public and
  open source with a licence file detectable and visible at the top of the repo
  page (About section); must contain the /.kiro directory at the ROOT of the
  project (specs, hooks, steering) — do NOT add /.kiro or sub-folders to
  .gitignore, as that could disqualify the submission. Skeleton Crew projects need
  2 separate repo folders for the 2 applications (not our category).
- URL to the working app(s). If login credentials are needed, add them in the
  Devpost submission form (judges only, not public).
- Text description explaining features and functionality.
- Demo video: under 3 minutes (judges not required to watch beyond 3 min); show
  the project working on the device it was built for; upload to YouTube, public;
  no third-party trademarks, copyrighted music, or other copyrighted material
  without permission.
- Write-up on how Kiro was used: which Kiro apps (IDE/CLI/Web/Crew), custom
  agents, agent hooks, spec-driven development, steering docs, MCP, property-based
  testing, Kiro powers. Judges must understand how effectively Kiro was used.
- Bonus Prize extras: Best Startup — confirm founder status + LinkedIn + startup
  website (only if genuinely eligible); Best Ghost of Kiroween Past — confirm
  2025 registration; Best Kiro Crew App — public URL to the Kiro Crew app repo;
  Social Blitz — social post with #KiroweenHackathon26 at the start, URL in the
  submission; Bonus Blog — public blog post about Kiro + the project on a
  third-party platform, URL in the submission.

## Judging criteria (from the live page)
- Potential Impact: credible case for a real problem for a real audience; useful,
  intuitive, accessible; would someone actually use it?
- Technological Implementation: how thoroughly and skillfully Kiro is used; a
  thoughtful AI-development strategy; a variety of Kiro tools used impactfully;
  deep understanding of Kiro features; experimentation.
- Design: complete, coherent product experience; intuitive, polished, intentional.
- Innovation/Idea: creative and novel concept; unusual technologies or public
  resources combined in a unique way.
- Presentation: video clearly demonstrates the project working end to end; the
  pitch communicates problem, audience, and why it matters; easy to follow;
  creative and memorable.
