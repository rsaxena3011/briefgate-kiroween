# Demo plan — BriefGate (for the parent to voice + approve)

Target: under 3 minutes, public YouTube, Strange Sightings channel ONLY.
Standing video gate applies: voiceover + very light background music under
the VO + boss approval of the final cut. No upload without all three.

## Owed assets (nothing here is recorded yet)

- **2-second GPT-generated start frame** (title card) — also the thumbnail.
  Must be GPT-generated; do NOT use a code-drawn card.
- **Voiceover recording** of the script below (default voice per standing
  rule: ElevenLabs web editor, "David - Audiobook & Documentary", Eleven v3
  — parent confirms the voice at record time).
- **Very light background music** under the VO, no copyrighted music
  (Devpost forbids copyrighted music without permission).
- **Boss approval of the final cut** before any upload.

## Motion-graphics plan (designed sequences, not static cards)

- MG-1 Title: the GPT start frame holds 2s, then a designed MG title
  reveal (BriefGate wordmark + "The brief is the truth." line) built as an
  animated sequence — not a static hold.
- MG-2 Pipeline: an animated 3-step diagram (Brief + Video → 3 checks →
  Evidence report) with each node lighting up as the VO names it.
- MG-3 Findings callouts: when a finding is shown, a designed MG callout
  (timestamp chip + severity tag) appears on a CUT, sits perfectly still,
  and leaves on a cut. **No moving/scaling overlays on footage, ever.**
- MG-4 Kiro montage: steering doc → spec → tasks → hook firing (screen
  capture of the lint + tests running), each on a cut, max 5s per shot.
- No Windows taskbar in any PC capture. Picture shows what the VO says,
  beat by beat; gaps between VO segments at most 2–3 seconds.

## VO script (draft, ~2:20 at conversational pace)

> [0:00–0:02, start frame] (no VO — title card)
>
> [0:02–0:10, anchor] "A client video and its brief go into BriefGate. Ten
> seconds later, the gate has already caught it — the wrong logo, at
> forty-two seconds, with the frame on screen next to the approved logo.
> Before any human watched the video through."
>
> [0:10–0:25, pain] "A wrong logo or a missing scene reaches the client, and
> the production house wears it. A manual QC pass takes hours. This gate
> takes under two minutes."
>
> [0:25–1:15, magic — live flow] "Upload the video, the brief, and the
> approved logo. The gate probes the video, finds the shots, and runs three
> checks. One: the logo check — every required shot against the approved
> reference. Two: the missing-scene check — the brief scene list, in order,
> against the detected shots. Three: technical QC — black frames, duration,
> silence, corrupt streams, all measured by FFmpeg. Every flag carries its
> timestamp and a screenshot of the actual frame."
>
> [1:15–1:45, decide + export] "A human approves or dismisses each flag —
> the gate reports, it never decides for you. Then the QC report exports as
> PDF for the client and JSON for the audit trail. Both agree with what you
> see on screen, because both come from the same stored records."
>
> [1:45–2:05, proof] "On our own planted-defect sample pack, the gate
> catches all four plants — the wrong logo, the missing scene, the
> three-second black segment, the silence — and the clean control video
> passes with zero findings. Full run: thirty-eight seconds."
>
> [2:05–2:20, Kiro + close] "It was built with Kiro: steering docs before
> code, spec-driven development, agent hooks, and a local MCP server — all
> in /.kiro at the repo root. BriefGate. The brief is the truth. The video
> has to prove it. Live demo and repo in the description."

## Shot list (capture against the live app — localhost:8000, sample pack)

1. Start frame (GPT) — 2s.
2. App open → upload sample_video.mp4 + brief.json + approved_logo.png →
   Run — real-time screen capture, no cuts inside the run.
3. Findings list appearing; click the logo_mismatch finding; evidence
   viewer with the NovaCorp frame beside the approved logo (show the logo
   PNG beside it in the edit, as a still comparison — the app shows the
   frame; the reference is overlaid as a designed still, not a moving overlay).
4. Approve the logo finding; dismiss the silence finding with its note.
5. Export PDF + JSON; open the PDF, page 1.
6. Control video run: 0 findings.
7. Kiro montage: `.kiro` tree in the repo, steering doc, specs folder,
   `scripts/lint_evidence.py` passing, pytest 10 passed.
8. Close card (MG): repo + app URLs.
