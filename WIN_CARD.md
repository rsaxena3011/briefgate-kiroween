## 7. Demo plan (video gate applies: VO plus light music plus boss approval)

Beats (target length under 3 minutes, per recon confirmation):
1. 0 to 2s: title frame (also the thumbnail). 2-second GPT-generated start frame per the standing rule.
2. First 10s: the anchor moment, working, no intro speech. Video and brief dropped in; the wrong logo at 0:43 is flagged with its frame beside the approved logo.
3. Pain: one line. A wrong logo or a missing scene reaches the client, and the production house wears it.
4. Magic: the core flow end to end. Ingestion, the three checks, findings with evidence, approve or dismiss, report exported.
5. Proof: metrics, logs, real output on screen. Planted-error scoreboard (planted vs caught), the /.kiro tree, a hook firing, specs becoming tasks.
6. Impact: who this helps and why it matters. Production houses and agencies shipping client video daily.

Standing video rules baked into this plan: the picture shows what the VO says, beat by beat; gaps between VO segments at most 2 to 3 seconds; no static hold longer than about 5 seconds; no Windows taskbar in any PC capture; all sample footage is our own, no real client footage.
